## Design handover

Use the existing DM Sans and Manrope typography. Preserve charcoal #161c18 and cream #f6f6ef. The new icon accent #ffe000 is a selected web approximation of the supplied logo yellow, rather than a formally supplied brand token. Use it on dark surfaces and dark text on yellow buttons. Decorative service imagery is conceptual, with HTML copy outside the image. Generated visuals represent service concepts, not verified product screenshots. Use a 3-column desktop service layout, 2 columns on tablet and 1 on mobile for seven cards. Give AI Team and Marketing their own entries. Update five-service references and remove the old optional-Marketing placement when integrating the copy. Features and channels are agreed per customer.
