# Power-Up Talent — Visual Design Directions & Selection Record

## Context & Objective

Following the website design upgrade brief, we developed and evaluated two visual directions for the three anchor pages — **Homepage (`/`)**, **Platforms Hub (`/platforms`)**, and **Sales & Intelligence (`/sales-intelligence`)** — before carrying the selected system across all 18 pages of the Power-Up Talent website.

Both directions preserve:
- The existing Power-Up Talent logo (`assets/logo-brand.png`) and charcoal, warm-white, and Power-Up yellow (`#ffe000` / `#edc752`) palette.
- The two primary homepage routes (**Explore Recruitment & Coaching** and **Explore AI & Business Platforms**) plus the non-competing confidential candidate route.
- Verified capability claims, readiness boundaries, and honest enquiry states.

---

## Direction A — British Industrial Editorial & Typographic Authority

- **Visual Character**: Inspired by British industrial trade journals, engineering specification sheets, and modern editorial layouts.
- **Key Elements**:
  - High-contrast charcoal (`#11181e`) and warm architectural paper (`#f8f7f3`) surfaces divided by crisp 1px rule lines (`#dce2e6`) and tabular index markers (`01 / RECRUITMENT`, `02 / PLATFORMS`).
  - Large, confident typographic hierarchy (`Manrope` headlines paired with italic serif accents and structured specification grids).
  - Editorial sector visual plates framing UK electrical wholesale, technical distribution, construction, and engineering contexts.
- **Strengths**: Immediate brand authority, effortless scannability on mobile (`390px`), and clear separation between employer recruitment/coaching and software platforms.
- **Trade-off**: Purely static editorial layouts show *what* we say about a platform, but require more reading to understand *how* an operator acts inside the workflow.

---

## Direction B — Guided Workflow & Single-Action Product Workbench

- **Visual Character**: Inspired by focused single-action interaction patterns (referencing Monkeytype's low-friction focus on one clear action and immediate state feedback).
- **Key Elements**:
  - Places interactive, synthetic-data product workbenches front and centre on the Platforms Hub and product pages.
  - Guides the visitor through three concrete scenarios (**Handle an enquiry**, **Follow up a quote**, **Hand over a won deal**) where clicking a single primary button immediately updates the sample queue, counters, and status badges with an accessible live announcement and a one-click reset.
  - Uses early signature UI mock-ups in the hero of each of the seven platform pages so a visitor sees the core action (e.g., overdue quote `#Q-104` in Sales & Intelligence, 30-day renewal flag in CRM, 4 run outcomes in Automation) without scrolling.
- **Strengths**: Explains what each platform does in seconds; eliminates abstract software jargon.
- **Trade-off**: If used without editorial framing, a software-only look can underplay Power-Up Talent's human-led executive search and commercial coaching heritage.

---

## Decision: Combined Hybrid Direction (British Industrial Editorial + Guided Workflow Previews)

We selected a **hybrid of Concept A and Concept B**, combining:
1. **Concept A's British Industrial Editorial Grid & Typography** for page structure, hero hierarchy, the 3-moment branch scenario centrepiece on the homepage, and the Recruitment, Coaching, Partnerships, About, and Contact pages.
2. **Concept B's Early Signature Product Visuals & Monkeytype-Inspired Guided Demo** on `/platforms`, `/dashboard`, and all seven platform pages (`/crm`, `/communications`, `/sales-intelligence`, `/business-delivery`, `/automation`, `/ai-team`, `/marketing-social-media`), with every preview explicitly badged as **`ILLUSTRATIVE PREVIEW · SAMPLE DATA`**.
