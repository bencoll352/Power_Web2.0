— THE EXCLUSIVITY MODEL

One company per area. One per industry. For as long as we work together.

Exclusivity is not a sales tactic — it is the operating model. It is the only honest way to promise a business our full network, our best people and our sharpest intelligence.

— HOW IT WORKS

Four commitments we put in writing.

Your territory and industry are defined at the start of the partnership and written into the agreement. We do not take a second mandate inside that boundary.

Defined boundary

Your area and industry are agreed explicitly — region, sector and competitor set — so there is no ambiguity about what exclusivity covers.

Closed to competitors

While the partnership runs, we decline search, coaching and intelligence work from competing businesses inside your boundary.

First refusal on talent

Every relevant performer we uncover in your sector and region is presented to you first. Nobody else sees that pipeline.

Confidential by default

Your hiring plans, salary structures, restructures and growth strategy never appear in another client's briefing.
