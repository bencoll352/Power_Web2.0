— CHALLENGES WE SOLVE

The eight problems that quietly cap your growth.

These are the issues owners and directors describe to us before the partnership starts — and what changes once it does.

THE CHALLENGE

The best people in your market never apply

The performers you actually want are employed, busy and invisible on job boards. You interview whoever is available, not whoever is best.

HOW WE SOLVE IT

AI-assisted market mapping identifies every relevant performer in your territory, then we approach them confidentially, under NDA, on the basis of their career — not your vacancy.

THE CHALLENGE

Hiring takes months and costs momentum

An empty leadership seat leaks margin, customers and team morale every week it stays open.

HOW WE SOLVE IT

A vetted shortlist for an executive brief in 14—21 days, average placement in 28 days, because the mapping work is already done before you brief us.

THE CHALLENGE

Great hires land in a team that can't match them

One strong performer cannot outrun a sales floor without discipline, a manager without coaching, or a pipeline nobody works.

HOW WE SOLVE IT

Coaching and Academy programmes lift the team around the hire: prospecting rhythm, objection handling, margin protection, branch leadership and accountability.

THE CHALLENGE

Your pipeline depends on someone remembering to prospect

Revenue becomes reactive. Quiet months follow busy ones with no explanation anyone can act on.

HOW WE SOLVE IT

CorePlatform's lead generation engine continuously discovers, enriches and scores new opportunities and pushes them into the pipeline — self-refilling, every day.

THE CHALLENGE

Missed calls are quietly losing you work

Inbound enquiries arrive while your team is on site, in a meeting or after hours, and go to a competitor instead of voicemail.

HOW WE SOLVE IT

The AI receptionist answers every call, captures intent, transcribes and summarises it, then stages the outcome for a human to approve before it touches your data.

THE CHALLENGE

Your commercial data lives in five disconnected tools

Nobody trusts the forecast, reporting is a weekly archaeology project, and handovers drop commitments.

HOW WE SOLVE IT

One system of record for accounts, contacts, deals, tasks, campaigns and conversations — with weighted forecasting leadership can actually plan around.

THE CHALLENGE

AI is either banned or used recklessly

Teams either miss the productivity gain entirely or paste confidential data into tools with no policy, oversight or audit trail.

HOW WE SOLVE IT

Practical AI coaching plus governed adoption: use cases, prompt libraries, approval points, acceptable-use policy and a full audit trail. Nothing automated without human sign-off.

THE CHALLENGE

Your recruiter also works for your competitor

The shortlist you paid for gets shown to the business down the road, and your market intelligence becomes theirs.

HOW WE SOLVE IT

Strict single-client policy per area, per industry. While we work with you, we are closed to your competitors.
